Who We Are

Subrahmanya Sadana
Premier League

SSPL is a cricket tournament proudly organised by the residents — past and present — of Subrahmanya Sadana, a PG accommodation in Mangaluru. What started as a spark among a group of young, energetic housemates has grown into a full-fledged annual cricket league.

We are a community of passionate cricket lovers — college students, working professionals, and alumni — all united by their time at Subrahmanya Sadana and their love for the game. Every season, old faces return and new ones step up, making SSPL more than just a tournament — it's a reunion, a rivalry, and a celebration.
