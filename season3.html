Rules & Regulations

Official rules for SSPL 2026. All participants must read and agree before registering.

🏏 Eligibility

This tournament is exclusively for players who are present or past members of SSB. Outsiders are not allowed to register for SSPL.
Venue: Padua College Ground, Mangaluru  |  Reporting Time: All teams must check-in by 7:00 AM.

1
General Rules
  • Format: League Stage + Knockout
  • League Matches: Each team will play 4 matches
  • Ball: Wilson 3 Tennis Ball (fresh balls used for each innings)
  • Bowling Style: Overarm only — illegal action = No Ball called by umpire
  • Team Size: 11 players (named before toss); Squad Size — maximum 15 players
2
Tournament Structure
  • League Stage: Each team plays 4 league matches
  • Semi Final 1: 1st place vs 4th place
  • Semi Final 2: 2nd place vs 3rd place
  • Final: Winners of Semi Final 1 & Semi Final 2
  • Tie — League Matches: Both teams receive 1 point
  • Tie — Knockout Matches: Super Over decides the winner
3
Overs & Bowling Limits
  • League & Knockout Matches: 5 overs per match
  • Knockout Matches: May increase to 6 overs if time permits
  • Final: 5 overs (may increase to 6–7 overs if time permits)
  • If time is limited due to unforeseen circumstances, overs may be reduced
  • 5 Overs Match: Minimum 4 bowlers (2+1+1+1)
  • 6 Overs Match: Minimum 4 bowlers (2+2+1+1)
  • 7 Overs Match: Minimum 4 bowlers (2+2+2+1)
4
Over Rate
  • Each innings must finish within the allotted time
  • 5 Overs — 20 Minutes  |  6 Overs — 25 Minutes  |  7 Overs — 30 Minutes
  • Slow over rate penalties may be imposed
  • Only 4 fielders allowed outside the circle after allotted time
  • For every 5 minutes of delay beyond that, fielders outside the circle reduced by one
5
Officials & Decisions
  • All matches will start on or before scheduled time
  • Team captains must ensure players are ready in time
  • If a team is not ready at match time, the match is awarded as a walkover to the opposition (2 points)
  • Match officials' and organizers' decisions are final
  • Any misconduct reported by officials may lead to team disqualification
  • Organizers reserve the right to resolve disputes
  • Rules may be modified if required due to circumstances
